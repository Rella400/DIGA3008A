Week 8: Designing a Humane and Ethical Internet: Putting People Before Profit

March 17, 2025

To me, a fair and ethical internet is one where the user’s well-being comes first. It’s not something that should be added later, it should be the main goal. I started to understand this better after learning about the work of Tristan Harris and the Center for Humane Technology. They believe that websites and apps should be designed to protect people’s mental health, not just to make money. Right now, a lot of the internet feels like a trap. It pulls people in with endless scrolling, annoying pop-ups, and smart systems (called algorithms) that decide what we see without asking us.

Week 9: Digital Inequality Is Social Inequality

March 24, 2025

In their article “Digital Inequalities and Why They Matter,” Robinson and others (2015) explain that digital inequality, like not having internet or tech skills, is not separate from other social problems like racism, sexism, or poverty. Instead, it’s closely connected to them. They argue that digital inequality should be taken just as seriously as issues related to race, gender, or class. After reading this, I started to see the internet differently, not just as a neutral tool, but as a space that reflects who has power and access.
