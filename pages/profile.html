About Me: 3D Modeling Journey

As a multidisciplinary digital artist and game designer, 3D modeling has become one of my favorite ways to bring imagination to life. My work in Blender focuses on storytelling through space, structure, and form — whether it's designing eerie abandoned carnival rides for a first-person narrative game, or creating detailed props and environmental elements that reflect mood and atmosphere. Every model I create is part of a bigger vision. I don’t just model objects — I build narratives. My projects often revolve around emotional themes, using texture, lighting, and composition to evoke memory, mystery, and connection. From rusted Ferris wheels to old mascot statues and haunted generator rooms, I put intention into every edge and bevel. I’m also deeply interested in how technical choices — like topology, UV mapping, and shading — affect both gameplay and user experience. I challenge myself to make models that are not only visually compelling but optimized for real-time engines like Unity. My portfolio reflects an evolving process: each project teaching me something new about lighting, structure, and how digital spaces speak to people. Whether you’re here to explore my models or simply curious about the process behind them, thank you for taking a look. Every asset I share is a piece of a story I’m still telling — one frame, one polygon at a time.
